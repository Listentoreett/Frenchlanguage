# Week 4 – Advanced Professional French

## Goal
By the end of this week, you should be able to:
- speak naturally in a professional French environment
- handle formal communication
- respond to job offers and follow-up messages
- present yourself in a polished and natural way
- sound confident in interviews, meetings, and daily workplace communication

## 8. Review checklist
You should be able to:
- sound polite and professional
- present yourself naturally in a French interview
- write a polished follow-up message
- explain motivation and skills confidently
- use business-level vocabulary and sentence patterns
