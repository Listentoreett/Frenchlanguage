# Week 2 – Daily Life and Workplace French

## Goal
By the end of this week, you should be able to:
- talk about daily life
- ask for directions and services
- speak in a workplace situation
- understand common professional phrases
- use polite and useful forms like “vous” and “pouvez-vous”
