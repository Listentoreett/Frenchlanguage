# Week 1 – Basic Survival French

## Goal
By the end of this week, you should be able to:
- greet people
- introduce yourself
- ask basic questions
- understand simple French in daily life
- use essential verbs in the present tense

> Pronunciation tip: Click the audio player beside each word or phrase to hear a French pronunciation example. Repeat it aloud several times.

## 7. Pronunciation tips

- French rhythm is important
- The “r” is often guttural
- “ou” sounds like “oo” in “food”
- “u” is different from English “you”
- Practice speaking in short phrases, not isolated words
