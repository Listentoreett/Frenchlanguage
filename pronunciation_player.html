French Pronunciation Coach

Listen to natural French phrases first. Then click Practice and speak the phrase out loud. Your pronunciation will be scored out of 10.

The best French pronunciation comes from your browser’s French voice. If your browser has no French voice installed, it will still sound close, but not perfectly native. Use Chrome or Edge for the best result.
