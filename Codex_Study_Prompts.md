# Codex Study Prompts for Learning French

Use these prompts with Codex to practice every day.

## Daily vocabulary prompts

### Prompt 1: Basic vocabulary
> “Create a French vocabulary list for everyday life and job search in Paris. Include 30 words with English meanings, pronunciation, and example sentences.”

### Prompt 2: Job vocabulary
> “Give me a list of 50 French words used in job applications, interviews, and workplace communication. Include definitions and sample sentences.”

### Prompt 3: Phrases for daily life
> “Give me 20 useful French phrases for daily conversations in Paris, including greetings, directions, shopping, and office situations.”

## Grammar prompts

### Prompt 4: Verb practice
> “Explain the present tense of être, avoir, aller, and faire with examples for job search and daily conversations.”

### Prompt 5: Politeness in French
> “Explain the difference between tu and vous with examples for interviews, customer service, and formal workplace communication.”

### Prompt 6: Pronunciation help
> “Teach me French pronunciation for difficult sounds such as r, u, on, an, ou, and eu. Give me practice sentences.”

## Speaking prompts

### Prompt 7: Self-introduction
> “Act as my French speaking coach. Ask me to introduce myself in French and correct my pronunciation and sentence structure.”

### Prompt 8: Recruiter roleplay
> “You are a French recruiter in Paris. Interview me for a job in [your field]. Ask me 10 questions in French and correct my answers naturally.”

### Prompt 9: Daily conversation
> “Create a 10-minute conversation in French about work, travel, and daily life. Then test me with questions and correct my responses.”

## Writing prompts

### Prompt 10: CV writing
> “Write a sample French CV for a candidate with [your experience]. Keep it professional and realistic for the Paris job market.”

### Prompt 11: Cover letter
> “Draft a professional French cover letter for a job in [industry]. Make it natural, formal, and convincing.”

### Prompt 12: Interview one-liners
> “Give me 20 strong French sentences for interviews, including introductions, strengths, motivation, availability, and follow-up.”

## Revision prompts

### Prompt 13: Weekly recap
> “Give me a 20-question French quiz on Week 1/2/3/4 topics, including vocabulary, grammar, and interview language.”

### Prompt 14: Mistake correction
> “Take these French sentences and correct them to sound natural and professional. Explain the improvements.”

### Prompt 15: 30-day challenge
> “Create a 30-day French challenge with daily tasks, speaking drills, vocabulary review, and interview practice for job search in Paris.”

## Suggested usage routine
- Morning: vocabulary and grammar review
- Midday: speaking roleplay
- Evening: interview practice and corrections

---

These prompts help you turn Codex into a French tutor, speaking coach, and interview trainer.
