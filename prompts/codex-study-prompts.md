# Tutor Prompts For French Practice

Use these prompts with a tutor or AI assistant after completing the daily lesson.

## Pronunciation

`Act as my French pronunciation coach. Give me five drills for /ʁ/, /y/, /u/, /ɔ̃/, /ɑ̃/, and /ɛ̃/. Correct my likely mistakes and give IPA for each word.`

`I will paste a French sentence. Break it into syllables, mark silent final consonants, mark liaison, and give me a shadowing plan.`

## Speaking

`Act as a polite French receptionist. Speak slowly. I need to ask for directions, repeat what I heard, and ask you to speak more slowly if needed.`

`Act as a French recruiter in Paris. Ask me one interview question at a time in French. After each answer, correct my grammar and make the sentence sound natural but still beginner-friendly.`

## Listening

`Create a short A1 French dialogue about finding an office. Give me the dialogue first, then five listening questions in English.`

`Give me a dictation exercise with five practical French sentences for daily life and job search. Show answers only after I try.`

## Writing

`Help me write a simple French CV profile for this background: [paste background]. Keep it professional, clear, and realistic.`

`Draft a short French cover-letter opening for this job: [paste job]. Use polite French and explain every sentence in English.`

## Weekly Review

`Quiz me on Week [1/2/3/4] of my French plan. Include vocabulary, grammar, listening-style questions, speaking prompts, and one writing task.`

`Review my French sentences below. Correct them, explain the errors, and give me a more natural version for a professional setting.`
